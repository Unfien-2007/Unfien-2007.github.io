Yesterday was world suicide prevention day. And i think i should try to journal my thoughts my way so im posting my feelings about suicide.

although im a young person only having 18 years in this world i think my current belief prevents me from physically trying to kill my self. not emotionally though, i still talk to my self in a self deprecating way.

when the pandemic happened the world kinda felt like nothing cares about you. we were not allowed to go outside, learning was hell, and talking to other people is kinda not happening.

at that time, the 13 year old me become bubbled by a world he cant understand, because of the pandemic my introverted self who can barely talk to other stopped all my f2f interaction with other altogether, my mind become entraped in an actual bad echochamber thinking about it now.

what i read at that time was stories about people dying and being reborn, and i wish that was me. at that time i would think to my self "How wonderfull would it be if i was reborn in a world that i can actually experience adventure". adventures that allows me to see something new, learn stories of other people, and solve puzzle at so called dungeons.

not only that but I think my idea of love have been stuck in what i read at that period of my life. meeting a person that will love you unconditionally... love you so much they would do anything for you... love you so much that all they think about is loving you... that if something... or someone try to get in the way... they would do anything just to be with you... my ideal person is like that, like a yandere type, not the one that physically hurt you though i think thats different.

My ideal Yandere is like i said love you so much they would do anything for you.
they cannot be violent in a way that they hurt you for a mistake like interacting with a girl in a public place, violent yandere like that dont actually love YOU, they just love the idea of loving someone, i categorize people like that as fake yandere. true yandere and the actual ideal yandere are people who makes sure you are always safe, comfortable, not hungry, always have proper amount of sleep, always clean, and always happy. but maybe that just what an actual relation ship is like, i dont know though, i have never been in a relationship and i have never felt a love other that love from people with ties on my blood.

its actually one of the reason I contemplate if i should kill my self every night, it makes me feel like i will probobly never meet someone who will love me. im a loser with self deprecating views about himself even though he dont show the not so confident self of his to the outside world. I have never feel like a candidate as someone to be romance, im not a person who proactively talks to other so meeting a girl like that is not possible. Im not particularly smart, always on the just above average grade, the grade that always pass but not remarkable, im not tall or handsome. fuck that i think im kind of ugly. standing on 153 cm, with an arch back due to osteoporosis. I hate looking at my self, it always make me sad and give me the feeling of wanting to cry.

But oh well we gotta stay positive right? we have to think about the future, a future where you can buy whatever you want, whatever you want to eat, and experince the things not posible in the past.

So lets get into how i stay positive even though every night i cry myself to sleep thinking about my future, the hardship i am experiencing in the moment, even though im the son of an alcoholic man who thankfully is not violent but like me is kinda pathetic in a way that he fails to keep a job due to his alcoholism, even though half the time im hungry when trying to sleep, and is scared that hes gonna die in his sleep due to his unoperated broken body he is born with.

I stay positive by thinking about how to accomplish my dream off having a job that i can earn a lot of money but my body wont do any physical labor, i stay positive by thinking "when i get a job, i can finally buy a computer i can play any games i can dream of", stay positive by listening to roleplay asmr where the girl talk to you with "affection", stay positive by learning about people less fortunate than my self so i can always remember to be thankful of my situation, stay positive by imagining that if i successfully graduate and earn a high paying job, i can finally be happy.

So even though imagining can be painful, it is less painful than thinking that ending it all is a solution to every problem you have. so never do it, just think about positive things. i would never say to you "pray to god" or "its okay". i think that is not effective, you should be the reason you are not killing your self just as the reason i dont kill myself even with what i am experiencing. you have to be the person that tells your self "I can do it".

thats everything positive i can think of to motivate myself not to kill myself. but there is also negative ways to do it, i think that killing yourself is a coward way to face life. life can be a onetime thing so you gotta take care of it.
